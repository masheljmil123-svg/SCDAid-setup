If CYP2D6 genotype is unavailable

Clinical indicators suggestive of altered CYP2D6 activity (informational only; does not replace genotyping):
  1. Past response: ineffective codeine/tramadol may suggest reduced CYP2D6 activation.
  2. Drug–drug interactions: strong CYP2D6 inhibitors may cause phenoconversion.
  3. Inflammation: acute illness may reduce drug-metabolizing activity.
These indicators support clinical reasoning only; they do not replace pharmacogenetic testing.
